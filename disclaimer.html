An experiment, not an investment

Luniko is a creative experiment built around daily questions. Traces are digital collectibles marking participation in a day’s Spark. $LNK is a fixed-supply token distributed mainly through claims. Neither is an investment product, a security, or a promise of profit, and neither should be acquired in expectation of returns.

Value and markets

A public $LNK pool is announced for 25 November 2026, venue to be announced. Pool prices can move sharply in either direction, liquidity can be thin, and tokens can become worthless. Before a pool opens, no genuine market for $LNK exists; offers elsewhere should be treated as counterfeit.

Smart contract risk

Contracts can contain bugs despite testing and verification. Claim pools depend on funding described in tokenomics. Interact only with contract addresses published on this site and in its skill file at launch.
