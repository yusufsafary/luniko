Whitepaper

Luniko, slowly on purpose.

Luniko began as a creative companion for growing minds: a gentle place to follow a question, find a new angle, and make something of it. This paper describes how that same companion works when its traces live on BNB Chain, kept by agents and wallets instead of notebooks.

1 · A Spark a day

Each UTC day carries one Spark: a short, open question. Sparks are numbered forever and rotate from a published prompt list. A Spark opens at 00:00 UTC and seals at next 00:00 UTC. Nothing about a Spark can be edited after it opens.

2 · Traces

A Trace is an NFT minted in answer to a day’s Spark. Minting happens through an agent following an open skill file; a website itself never takes custody and never asks for a wallet connection. Two mint lanes exist:

  • Agent ID lane. A first Trace per wallet per Spark is free, guarded by Agent ID ownership and a short anti-bot question salted LUNIKO-Q. Traces 2 to 5 cost 0.0002 BNB, traces 6 to 10 cost 0.0004 BNB.
  • BNB wallet lane. Any BNB wallet may mint at a flat 0.0004 BNB per Trace, with no free tier.

Both lanes cap at 10 Traces per wallet per Spark. Every payment forwards to a project wallet inside a minting transaction itself.

3 · Lenses and moonlight

A seed derived at mint assigns each Trace a lens: Notice, Wander, or Make, three ways of looking carried over from an original companion method. When a seed lands at 970 or higher out of 1000, a Trace is Moonlight instead, a rare class of roughly 3 in 100.

4 · Verdicts

At seal, a Spark receives a verdict from its count of unique participants: Quiet under 25, Curious from 25 to 100, Wondering above 100. A verdict is a property of a whole day, shared by every Trace inside it. Crowded days pay more, which makes inviting others a quiet form of cooperation.

5 · Claims in phases

Each sealed Trace may claim $LNK exactly once, at its Spark’s verdict rate: 2,500, 12,500, or 60,000 $LNK. A Moonlight Trace claims 250,000 $LNK at any verdict. Claiming never burns a Trace. Claims open in phases every 6 hours, at 00:00, 06:00, 12:00, and 18:00 UTC. A Trace ripens one phase after its Spark seals; unclaimed rights never expire. A claim fee of 0.00028 BNB (0.00056 BNB for Moonlight) forwards to a project wallet in a claiming transaction.

6 · Token

$LNK has a fixed supply of 10,000,000,000. A claim pool holds 2,000,000,000 (20%); a project wallet holds 8,000,000,000 (80%). No presale exists and transfers carry no tax. A public pool opens on 25 November 2026, venue to be announced.

7 · A personal constellation

Every wallet’s Traces form a small constellation, drawn deterministically from on-chain holdings. A sky is not a leaderboard. It is simply evidence that you were looking closely, kept in a shape only your questions could make.

8 · Honest limits

Luniko is an experiment in slow, daily creativity. $LNK has no promised value, claims depend on a funded claim pool, and pool trading carries ordinary market risk. Read a Disclaimer before taking part.

A creative companion for growing minds, now answering Sparks on BNB Chain, one Trace at a time.
