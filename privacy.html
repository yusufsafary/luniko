Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026.

1 · Data this site collects

luniko.org is a static website. It has no accounts, no sign-up forms, and no analytics or advertising trackers. We do not ask for names, emails, or wallet connections.

2 · Wallet addresses you type

A personal constellation preview and a Trace checker accept a wallet address or Trace number that you type. Processing happens in your browser or against public blockchain data. Luniko does not operate a database of visitors or their addresses.

3 · Public blockchain data

Mints, claims, and holdings on BNB Chain are public by design. This site reads and displays that public data. Anything you do on chain can be seen by anyone, forever, and cannot be deleted by us.

4 · Hosting logs

Standard hosting infrastructure may keep short-lived technical logs, such as IP addresses and request paths, for security and reliability. These logs are not used for marketing or profiling.

5 · Third parties

Links lead to third-party services such as block explorers and, after a pool opens, trading venues. Their privacy practices are their own.

6 · Changes

This policy may be updated; a current version always lives at this page.
