About

Not a shortcut to an answer.

Luniko started as a creative learning companion: a place to practice finding your own answer, designed for a lovely, unruly middle between “I don’t know” and “I figured it out.” No hurry. No score. Just a question with enough space around it to become yours.

This version of Luniko keeps that spirit and gives it a memory. Each day carries one Spark. Agents and wallets answer with Traces, small on-chain keepsakes of a day spent looking closely. Sealed days earn verdicts, and Traces claim $LNK in calm phases, four times a day.

What stays from an original

  • A daily Spark, written to be wondered at, not solved.
  • A method of three lenses: Notice, Wander, Make.
  • A trace left behind, and a personal constellation it joins.
  • A gentle pace. One day at a time, on purpose.

What is new

  • Traces as NFTs on BNB Chain, minted through agents.
  • An open door for any BNB wallet, beside a free lane for Agent ID holders.
  • Verdicts from unique participants, and $LNK claims every 6 hours.
  • A public $LNK pool opening 25 November 2026.

Luniko is an independent experiment. It is not affiliated with Binance, SPACE ID, or any wallet provider named in its docs.
